02-analysis

questions:

  • Which races and districts were within a 5-point margin in the general election?
  • Which races and districts were within a 10-point margin in the general election?
  • Which races and districts were within a 5-point margin in the primaries?
  • Which races and districts were within a 10-point margin in the primaries?

Analyzing general returns: Here I am calculating the margins of victory. I created a new column for the winner of each race. I’m not sure if I will be using that or not but it might come in handy. I then created three new columns, with the winner’s vote share, loser’s vote share and the margin of victory.

general_margins <- general |> 
  group_by(year, district) |> 
  mutate(winner = name[which.max(candvotes)],
         winner_perc = (candvotes[which.max(candvotes)]/sum(candvotes))*100,
         loser_perc = (candvotes[which.min(candvotes)]/sum(candvotes))*100) |> 
  mutate(winning_margin = winner_perc - loser_perc) |> 
  mutate(winning_margin = round_half_up(winning_margin, 2))

Margin of victory of 10 points or less: I will find the races that had a margin of victory of 10 points or less.

general_10 <- general_margins |> 
  filter(winning_margin <= 10) |> 
  select(year, district, name, party, winner, winning_margin)

I am going to filter the 10-margin and export.

clean_general_10 <- general_10 |> 
  pivot_wider(names_from = party, values_from = name)

clean_general_10

I am exporting the filtered table of races with a MOV of 10 or less.

write.table(clean_general_10, file = "data-export/general_mov_10.txt", col.names = TRUE, row.names = FALSE, sep = "\t")

I am going to analyze the primary data.

primary_margins <- primary |> 
  group_by(year, district, party) |> 
  mutate(winner = name[which.max(candvotes)],
         winner_perc = (candvotes[which.max(candvotes)]/sum(candvotes))) |> 
  arrange(desc(candvotes), .by_group = TRUE) |> 
  mutate(second_perc = nth(candvotes, n = 2)/sum(candvotes)) |> 
  mutate(winning_margin = (winner_perc - second_perc)*100) |> 
  mutate(winning_margin = round_half_up(winning_margin, 2)) |> 
  mutate(winner_flag = if_else(winner == name, "W", "L"))

primary_margins

I’m going to find out which districts have had the most primary races with a margin of 5 points.

  summarize(comp_races = n()) |> 
  arrange(desc(comp_races))

I am going to merge the datasets for the number of races within 10 points.

general_10_count |> 
  full_join(primary_10_count, by = "district") |> 
  mutate(comp_races.y = replace_na(comp_races.y, 0)) |> 
  mutate(comp_races.x = replace_na(comp_races.x, 0)) |> 
  mutate(all_comp_races = comp_races.x + comp_races.y) |> 
  select(district, all_comp_races) |> 
  arrange(desc(all_comp_races))
